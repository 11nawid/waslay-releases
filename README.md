## 📱 About Waslay

Waslay connects people through real-world social events, community activities, and spontaneous meetups. 

This repository hosts official, pre-compiled Android application packages (`.apk`) for sideloading and Over-The-Air (OTA) in-app updates.

---

## 📥 How to Install

1. Go to the [**Releases**](https://github.com/11nawid/waslay-releases/releases) page.
2. Download the latest `.apk` file (e.g. `waslay-release.apk`).
3. Open the downloaded file on your Android device.
4. If prompted, enable **"Allow installation from this source"** in your device settings.
5. Tap **Install** to complete setup.

> [!NOTE]
> **Existing Users:** Installing a new update over an existing version preserves all your messages, account credentials, and saved data. You will **not** be logged out.

## 🔄 In-App Auto-Updates

Waslay includes a built-in Over-The-Air (OTA) update system:
- When a new version is published here, your app will automatically notify you at the top of your feed.
- Tap **Update Now** inside the app to download and upgrade seamlessly in the background.
